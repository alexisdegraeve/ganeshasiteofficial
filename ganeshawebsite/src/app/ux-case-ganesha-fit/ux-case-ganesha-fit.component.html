Scenario

Ganesha Fit is a web application concept for a fitness club. It helps users explore gym facilities, join classes, manage subscriptions, and book personal coaching sessions. Designed for simplicity and practical UX, it ensures users can quickly find what they need.

User

Who: Gym member / fitness enthusiast. Someone who wants to explore gym facilities, join classes, manage subscriptions, and contact a coach.

Needs

  • See the different machines available at the gym
  • Browse group classes
  • Manage their subscription
  • Book a personal coach
  • Find a club nearby
  • Contact the gym or staff

Acceptance Criteria / UX Notes

  • Machines → Filter by type, highlight availability
  • Classes → Responsive schedule, clear signup process
  • Subscription → Update plan easily, confirmation of changes
  • Coach → Book sessions with confirmation and calendar integration
  • Find a club → Search bar + map view, responsive
  • Contact → Form or direct email/phone link, easy to access
