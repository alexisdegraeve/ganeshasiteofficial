Scenario

Ganesha Music is a responsive web-based music streaming platform inspired by modern audio services. It allows users to explore artists, listen to live radio streams, watch music videos, and choose a membership based on their budget and listening habits. The platform focuses on accessibility, simplicity, and immersive audio experience.

User

Who: Music lovers who enjoy discovering artists, listening to live radio, watching music videos, and managing subscriptions easily across mobile and desktop devices.

Needs

  • Discover artists and browse music easily
  • Listen to live radio streaming
  • Search and switch between radio stations
  • Save favorite radio stations
  • Watch music videos on desktop
  • Choose a membership adapted to their budget

Acceptance Criteria

  • Menu → Clear navigation with footer always accessible
  • Artists → Search and alphabetical filtering
  • Live → Play/pause, next station, waveform visualization
  • Favorites → Save up to 5 radio stations
  • Videos → Desktop-focused layout with search and artist filter
  • Memberships → Clear pricing, visual hierarchy, WCAG-compliant differentiation

Memberships

Three subscription plans are available: Comfort, Premium, and Ultimate. Trophies of different sizes are used to visually differentiate plans, ensuring accessibility for users with visual impairments, in compliance with WCAG guidelines.

Test

Users provided feedback which was incorporated. They are satisfied, and we can proceed to high-fidelity mockups.

Conclusion

Ganesha Music demonstrates a user-centered design approach focused on accessibility, clarity, and immersive music experience. The platform balances simplicity and functionality, making it easy for users to enjoy music across devices while managing their subscriptions effortlessly.
